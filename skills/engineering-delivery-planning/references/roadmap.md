# Outcome roadmaps

Start from the selected direction and source need. Record whose outcome changes,
how success/learning will be observed, the relevant horizon and confidence. A roadmap
is not a release promise or a requirement-level feature inventory.

Use horizons meaningful to the project (for example Now/Next/Later). A specific date
is a forecast unless an actual authority has agreed its scope/basis. State unknown
confidence, target or owner honestly. Keep desired measure distinct from current
observed baseline; missing baseline is a learning task, not a manufactured number.

Record dependencies, uncertainties and review trigger. Triggers may be a measured
outcome, unresolved decision, dependency event or newly observed constraint. Review
when evidence changes rather than moving dates without explaining why.

Trace outcomes to justified needs, then to bounded milestones. Show commitment
status and agreement evidence independently of proposed priority. Do not convert a
planner's subjective score or estimated effort into a probability or guarantee.

If the question is which direction to choose, use next-milestone-decision. Once
selected, this skill records the plan without executing it. Use Roadmap records and
full source rationale; all audiences retain material uncertainty. Basis:
[research/plan](../../../docs/skills/engineering-artifact-catalogue/PLAN.md), S09/S10/S14/S23.
