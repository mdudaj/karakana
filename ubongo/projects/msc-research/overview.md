# MSc Research Overview

The `msc-research` skillpack is retained for manuscript-oriented research
writing. `docs/skillpacks.md` distinguishes it from `msc-platform`, which owns
the `stemgen-platform` prototype and evaluation software. No manuscript
repository or owner was identified in the available project records on
2026-10-01. Do not use the platform checkout as a substitute manuscript source.

Use `research-writing` when the task concerns methodology, literature,
results or manuscript prose, after obtaining the actual manuscript and source
material for that task.
