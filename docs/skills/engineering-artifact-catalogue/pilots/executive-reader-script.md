# Fictional executive workbook review — reader handout

This review uses a fictional local checklist-reader project. We are evaluating whether the workbook helps someone find decisions and their evidence. We are not testing you. Use the supplied workbook and any linked source files as you normally would. Please think aloud, including when something is unclear. You may say that you cannot find an answer, and you may stop at any time. Do not edit the supplied files.

Work through these tasks in order. For each answer, show where you found it in the workbook and, when asked, the underlying source. The facilitator will record your path and answers but will not guide you to a sheet or cell.

1. **Release decision (T1).** Would you advise proceeding with the fictional release today? Explain the evidence behind your advice and show the sources you used.
2. **Next decision or evidence (T2).** Find one decision or piece of evidence someone should seek next. Show where the workbook says this and open its underlying source.
3. **Plan and actual result (T3).** What does this pack say is planned, and what has actually been verified or delivered? Explain how you can tell.
4. **Trace a claim (T4).** Choose one statement about a release status or risk. Find its complete source and show how you identified the source and its revision. Explain the relationship between that source and the workbook.

At the end, describe anything that made the decision, status or source hard to find. A missing or unclear answer is useful feedback.
