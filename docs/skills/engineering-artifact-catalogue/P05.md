machine contract fixture. Do not copy P03 prose or instantiate the global blank

semantics, committed share-pack parity and reviewed revision history. These protect
pilot outcomes; deterministic source-text evals alone do not prove authoring quality.
Run baseline evals before guidance changes, focused checks then required isolated
full pytest/evals and skill/skillpack validation. Inspect final source/share semantics,
document remaining gaps and safely regenerate unannotated research continuation.

## Delivered outcomes and verification

The [pilot reading/sharing guide](pilots/README.md) contains the new resolved brief,
four authored documents and machine fixtures. Original pack: 49 structured records;
reviewed amendment: 50. All documents stay draft, ADR Proposed, priorities/owners
unconfirmed and product runs Not run/Blocked. No delivered product release note is
invented. The small 0.2 amendment clarifies matching already resolved in the brief,
retains all IDs/history and has its own derived business export.

Six reviewed XLSX/JSON pairs are accompanying audience documentation assets:
five original profiles plus amended business. Each baseline covers seven source
files. [QA](pilots/QA.md) and its exact hash/UTC observation record distinguish
actual documentation/office outcomes from fictional product behavior.

- 14 functional pilot checks pass: source/authority/cross-file validity, observable
  criteria/cases, five profile semantics, six committed pair/source/link/QA-hash
  comparisons, revision history and stale/conflicting feedback without writes.
- Six actual feedback exercises retain comments/formulas/additional content and
  report competing values, removal conflicts, source staleness and changed criteria.
  All original and returned bytes preserved; current-source simulation isolated.
- LibreOffice 26.2.5.2 opened/saved all six final views: zero semantic changes,
  zero stale/source issues. Executive PDF rendered; readiness pages 5/6 visually
  inspected, with unverified release and test blockers visible.
- Full isolated regression: 676 passed in 24.09s. Baseline/delivery deterministic
  evals: 171 passed, zero failures/warnings. Skills/packs and project memory valid;
  three known non-target missing-memory warnings in the isolated copy only.
- Supported module CLI validates/exports original and amended packs. Generic blank
  assets, skills/statuses, index, packs, routing and native engines remain unchanged.
  Prior research pack checked for parity/no annotations before archival; current
  Markdown/JSON/XLSX version 0.6 preserves 15 sheets/226 research records.

The pilot found no blocker requiring a parser/profile change. Initial machine
